Overview

Talent Mapping & Market Intelligence Reports means collecting and analysing information about talent availability, competitors’ leadership teams, and salary trends in a particular industry or role.

What we do

This helps companies make better hiring and workforce planning decisions — with evidence rather than assumptions.

  • Evidence-based hiring
  • Competitor insight
  • Salary benchmarks

Explanation

Two lenses on your talent market

01

Talent Mapping

Identifying key professionals, leaders, and skilled talent available in the market for specific roles or industries.

02

Market Intelligence

Providing insights about competitor organisations, hiring trends, salary benchmarks, and talent movement in the market.

What Our Reports Include

Actionable insight, clearly presented

01

Talent Pool Mapping

Profiles of relevant professionals by role, function, location and experience.

02

Competitor Leadership Structures

How peer organisations structure and staff their leadership teams.

03

Compensation Benchmarks

Salary and reward trends for target roles and industries.

04

Talent Movement Trends

Where talent is moving from and to, and why.

05

Hiring Trends

Demand signals, emerging roles and skills in your market.

06

Succession Pipelines

Internal and external candidates for future critical roles.
