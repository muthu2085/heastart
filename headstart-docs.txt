4 Talent Mapping & Market Intelligence Reports

Talent Mapping & Market Intelligence Reports means collecting and analyzing information about talent availability, competitors’ leadership teams, and salary trends in a particular industry or role. This helps companies make better hiring and workforce planning decisions.
Explanation
Talent Mapping – Identifying key professionals, leaders, and skilled talent available in the market for specific roles or industries.


Market Intelligence – Providing insights about competitor organizations, hiring trends, salary benchmarks, and talent movement in the market.
